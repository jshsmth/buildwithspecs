# Change handoff

## Changed
What changed and which acceptance criteria it satisfies.

## Verified
Actual commands or checks, their results, and what they establish.

## Review
Confirmed findings, repairs, and unresolved issues.

## Delivery status
Report separately: local checks, hosted CI, PR/merge, deployment, and post-deployment acceptance. Use Not performed or Blocked when appropriate.

## Project knowledge
Which current contracts or decisions were updated.

## Remaining work
Specific blockers, required actions, and who or what can resolve them.
