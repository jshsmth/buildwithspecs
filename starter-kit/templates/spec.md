# Specification: feature name

## Problem
The user's problem and current behaviour.

## Intended behaviour
The outcome, including applicable failure and recovery states.

## Scope and non-goals
The explicit boundary of this change.

## Decisions and constraints
Accepted decisions; links to existing contracts.

## Acceptance criteria
Numbered, observable outcomes.

## Verification
Map each criterion to a test, inspection, or acceptance check.

## Open decisions
Unresolved questions, without presenting assumptions as facts.
