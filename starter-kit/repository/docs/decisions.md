# Decisions

Record accepted decisions here or link to individual decision records when this page grows. Keep proposals visibly separate from accepted decisions.

## Decision record

- Title and status: proposed, accepted, or superseded.
- Context: the problem and constraints.
- Decision: the chosen approach and why.
- Consequences: tradeoffs, rejected alternatives, and follow-up work.
- Evidence: relevant code, tests, or research.
- Supersedes: the earlier decision, if applicable.
