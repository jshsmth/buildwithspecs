# Illustrative final handoff

This is an example of the report structure after all three tickets are completed. No application checks or deployment were actually performed for this example.

## Changed
Daily-reminder preferences persist across sessions, are enforced by the scheduler, and can be changed in Settings. Account and security messages are unchanged.

## Checks to record in a real report
- Commands and results for ownership, validation, defaults, persistence, and scheduler behaviour.
- Commands and results for loading, failed-save rollback, retry, and keyboard interaction.
- Browser or device and observations for cross-session acceptance.

## Review outcome to record
Confirmed findings, any fixes made, and the checks rerun after those fixes.
